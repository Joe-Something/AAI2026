# Printer and scanning problems

All office printers use secure release printing ("Follow-Me Print"). You print to the queue `BAYVIEW-SECURE` and release the job at any printer by tapping your badge.

## Job does not print
1. Confirm you selected the `BAYVIEW-SECURE` printer, not a printer from a previous office.
2. Tap your badge on the printer panel and look for the job in the list. Jobs are held for 24 hours.
3. If your badge is not recognized, register it once: tap the badge, sign in with your username and password on the panel.

## Adding the printer
- Windows: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners > Add device > `BAYVIEW-SECURE`.
- If it does not appear, connect to VPN or the office network and run Company Portal > Sync.

## Paper jams and toner
- Follow the on-screen diagram. For toner or repeated jams, report the printer's asset tag (sticker on the front) so facilities can service it.

## Scan to email
- Scans are sent to your work email. Check Junk if it does not arrive within 5 minutes.
