# Reporting phishing and security incidents

Security incidents are always handled by a human analyst on the Security Operations team. Report quickly; you will not get in trouble for reporting.

## Suspicious email (phishing)
- Do not click links, open attachments, or reply.
- Use the **Report Phish** button in Outlook. This sends the message to Security and removes it from your inbox.

## If you clicked a link or entered your password
1. Reset your password immediately (KB-001) from a different, trusted device if possible.
2. Report it to the Service Desk as a security incident and say what you clicked and when.
3. Watch for unexpected MFA prompts and deny them.

## Lost or stolen device
- Report within 1 hour so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. Include the time and place it was lost.

## Other incidents
- Ransomware messages, files suddenly renamed or encrypted, or antivirus alerts: disconnect from the network (turn off Wi-Fi, unplug the cable) and call the Service Desk immediately. Do not power off the device.
