# Service Desk priorities, SLAs and escalation

| Priority | Definition | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Security incident, outage affecting many users, or business-critical system down | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | One user fully blocked from working, or VIP user affected | 1 hour | 8 business hours |
| P3 Medium | Degraded service with a workaround available | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Requests, questions, and how-to | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Queues
- **Service Desk L1**: accounts, passwords, how-to, software requests.
- **Desktop Support L2**: hardware, printers, laptop rebuilds.
- **Network Ops**: Wi-Fi, VPN gateways, outages.
- **Security Ops**: phishing, compromised accounts, lost devices. Always human-handled.
- **Identity & Access**: access requests and MFA resets requiring identity verification.

## Escalation rules
- The IT Assistant escalates to a human when it cannot answer from the knowledge base, when an automated fix fails, when the user asks for a person, or for any security-related issue.
- Escalated tickets include a summary, steps already tried, and the conversation transcript so the user does not have to repeat themselves.
