# Requesting access to systems, shared drives and mailboxes

Access follows the principle of least privilege: you get what your role needs, approved by the data owner.

## How to request
1. Tell the IT Assistant which system, folder or mailbox you need and why.
2. A ticket is created and routed to the owner for approval (for example, the Finance share is owned by the Finance Director).
3. After approval, access is granted within 1 business day. Sign out and back in for group changes to apply.

## New hires
- Managers submit onboarding requests at least 5 business days before the start date. Standard access (email, Wi-Fi, Company Portal, HR portal) is created automatically.

## Elevated or sensitive access
- Administrator rights, production databases, payroll and customer data require IT Security approval in addition to the data owner, and are reviewed every quarter.

## Removing access
- Managers must request removal when an employee changes role. Departing employees lose access automatically at 5 PM on their last day.
