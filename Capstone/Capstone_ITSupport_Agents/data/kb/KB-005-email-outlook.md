# Outlook and email issues

## Outlook will not open or keeps asking for a password
1. Close Outlook completely, then open it again.
2. If prompted repeatedly for a password, your password may have changed: sign out of Office (File > Office Account > Sign out), restart, and sign in again.
3. Start Outlook in safe mode (`outlook.exe /safe`) to rule out a broken add-in. Disable add-ins under File > Options > Add-ins if safe mode works.

## Emails not arriving
- Check the **Junk Email** and **Other** (Focused Inbox) folders.
- Check whether your mailbox is full: the default quota is 50 GB. Archive or delete large items.
- External senders may be blocked by the spam filter; ask IT to review quarantine.

## Shared mailboxes and distribution lists
- Access to shared mailboxes must be approved by the mailbox owner; submit an access request (KB-010).

## Mobile mail
- Use the Outlook mobile app. Native mail apps are not supported and are a common cause of account lockouts (KB-002).
