# Setting up or replacing your MFA device

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, VPN, SSO and self-service password reset.

## First-time setup
1. Install **Microsoft Authenticator** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. On a computer, sign in to https://mysignins.bayview.example and choose **Security info > Add method > Authenticator app**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app and approve the test notification.
4. Add a phone number as a backup method.

## New or lost phone
- If you still have the old phone: add the new phone as a method first, then remove the old one.
- If the old phone is lost or wiped: you cannot reset MFA yourself. Contact the Service Desk; an agent must verify your identity (employee ID plus manager confirmation) before clearing MFA methods. Lost company phones must also be reported as a security incident (KB-011).

## Tips
- Do not approve MFA prompts you did not start. Unexpected prompts mean someone may know your password: reset it immediately (KB-001) and report it (KB-011).
